About me

I design backend systems that have to stay correct when things go wrong: requests that span many services, many tenants, and many approval steps, without losing track of a single one.

Most of my recent work sits where identity security meets platform engineering, at Saviynt in Los Angeles. I like owning a system end to end — from the architecture review to the production fix — and helping the people around me grow through design reviews, code reviews, and mentoring.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Cal State East Bay. Outside work, I judge hackathons and enjoy talking with other engineers about distributed systems.
