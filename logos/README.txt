Put logo files in this folder. Any logo you add appears on the site automatically;
any logo you skip keeps the colored letter badge.

Company and school logos (PNG, square works best, about 200x200 px):
  logos/saviynt.png
  logos/amdocs.png
  logos/cybage.png
  logos/tcs.png
  logos/csueb.png      (Cal State East Bay)
  logos/pict.png       (Pune Institute of Computer Technology)
  logos/lahacks.png    (shown on the LA Hacks event card)

Tech icons (SVG, small and square) go in logos/tech/, named after the skill:
  java.svg, sql.svg, groovy.svg, spring-boot.svg, spring-security.svg, hibernate.svg,
  kafka.svg, rabbitmq.svg, temporal.svg, postgresql.svg, elasticsearch.svg, redis.svg,
  mysql.svg, aws.svg, azure.svg, kubernetes.svg, docker.svg, helm.svg,
  gitlab-ci-cd.svg, jenkins.svg, oauth2.svg, jwt.svg

Tip: many projects publish official logo files on their own websites (often on a
"brand" or "press kit" page). Check each one's usage guidelines.
